# Short Bio

I’m an applied computational R&D scientist with hands-on experience in developing high-performance computing (HPC) solutions, AI models, and simulation tools for industrial and research challenges.

Currently, I work at the CReSTIC Laboratory in Reims, France, where I lead the development of real-time fault detection software for industrial automation. My work involves parallelizing Python code with CUDA, managing large-scale jobs via Slurm, and deploying machine learning models in production pipelines that meet real-time performance requirements.

Previously, I collaborated with Constellium and the SIMaP Laboratory in Grenoble to enhance the understanding of aluminum alloy behavior under industrial processing conditions. With a strong command of Linux environments, Bash scripting, Git, Docker, Streamlit, and custom Python workflows, I apply data-driven approaches to complex problems, delivering actionable insights to engineering teams.

I’m looking forward to contributing to R&D-focused teams where performance, precision, and innovation are essential.
