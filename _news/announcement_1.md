I am thrilled to share that I received a **Certificate of Distinction for Excellence in Teaching** during the International Teaching Week at **Hochschule Hof, University of Applied Sciences**. This achievement marks an important milestone in my professional journey, and I am truly grateful for the knowledge, experiences, and opportunities it has brought me.

A special thank you to **Prof. Dr.-Ing. Valentin Plenk** and everyone at the university for their support and guidance. This experience has opened many exciting avenues for collaboration between **CReSTIC – Université de Reims Champagne-Ardenne** and **Hochschule Hof**, paving the way for innovative research and academic growth — particularly in our contributions to the **DoRéMi Project**, led by **Dr. Ramla SADDEM**.

It was a pleasure to connect with both international and German students and introduce them to the fundamentals of **Artificial Intelligence** and **Parallel Computing**. Seeing their enthusiasm and curiosity for these topics was incredibly rewarding.
